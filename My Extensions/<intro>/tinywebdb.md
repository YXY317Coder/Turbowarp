# 拓展使用教程
1. 在 [TinywebDB官网](https://tinywebdb.appinventor.space/) 注册账号并登录

Register an account on [TinywebDB]((https://tinywebdb.appinventor.space/)) official website and log in

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/17ad24b4-f58b-4eff-99d8-11a2f181b2d0)

2. 找到自己的 `user` 和 `secret`，请牢记它，下面要用。
   
Find your own `user` and `secret`, please remember it.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0ff799cb-e856-4fda-a98f-3445ccc2c457)

3. 填入初始化积木后，就可以开始使用啦！
   
After filling in your own `user` and `secret` into the initialization block, you can start using it.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16ffd7e8-f55a-4716-bd78-0c2ae3087f4f)

4. 还可以点击登陆后的页面的“数据浏览”，查看云数据哦！
   
You can also click on the "Data Browse" button on the login page to view cloud data.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/131ead12-6297-4d6f-b91c-d38a6ff82063)

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/08fcfa33-4c47-4f69-860a-47217c7dea3c)
